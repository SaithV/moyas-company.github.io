📱 Moya’s Company — Phone Repair & Accessories

Moya’s Company is a simple and modern static website built for a local phone repair and accessories business in Nogales, Sonora, Mexico.
The website showcases the store’s services, products, repair process, location, and contact information — making it easier for customers to find and connect with the business online.

🌐 Live Demo: https://saithvega.github.io/moyas-company

🧾 Overview

This project was created to help a local business establish a professional online presence.
The goal was to design a clean, responsive, and informative website that reflects Moya’s Company’s friendly and trustworthy service style.

The site includes:
		🔧 Repair services — information about phone and tablet repairs.
		🛍️ Products — accessories and gadgets available in-store.
		🕒 Business hours & contact info — easy to find and mobile-friendly.
		📍 Google Maps integration — helps customers locate the shop.

This project represents how web design can help small local businesses connect with their community and attract more customers.
